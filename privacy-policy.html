Privacy Policy for Cora

Last Updated: January 2025

Introduction

Cora ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information.

Cora is designed to be completely offline and privacy-focused:

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • No data is sent to our servers
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No third-party data sharing
  • No cloud synchronization

Information Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your device only:

  • Crisis plans you create
  • Emergency contacts
  • Medication information
  • Journal entries
  • App preferences and settings

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

Data Export

You can export your data at any time through the Settings screen. This creates a JSON file that you can save, share, or backup as you choose. We have no access to this exported data.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time using the "Clear All Data" option in Settings. This permanently removes all information from your device.

Permissions

Cora requests the following permissions:

  • Notifications: To send medication reminders and weekly crisis plan check-ins. You can disable these at any time in Settings.
  • Phone: To allow you to call emergency contacts directly from the app.

These permissions are optional and the app will function without them (though some features may be limited).

Third-Party Services

Cora does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Cora is not intended for children under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@outrelink.com

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • You have complete control over your data
  • You can export your data at any time
  • You can delete your data at any time
  • You can uninstall the app to remove all data

Your privacy is our priority. Your data belongs to you.
